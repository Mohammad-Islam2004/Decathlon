# 🛍️ Decathlon Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

> An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze Decathlon's sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and business KPIs. The dashboard provides actionable insights through dynamic visualizations, executive KPI cards, and AI-driven narrative insights to support data-driven decision making.

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard transforms raw retail sales data into meaningful business insights. It enables stakeholders to monitor sales performance, customer trends, product category performance, regional contribution, and operational metrics through an intuitive and interactive interface.

The report is fully dynamic and responds to user selections such as **Year** and **Quarter**, making it suitable for executive reporting and business analysis.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall business performance.
- Track sales and customer KPIs.
- Identify top-performing product categories and regions.
- Monitor customer retention trends.
- Compare sales across multiple dimensions.
- Provide dynamic business insights using DAX.

---

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 💰 Total Sales | Total revenue generated |
| 🛒 Total Orders | Total number of orders placed |
| 👥 Total Customers | Unique customers served |
| 💳 Average Order Value (AOV) | Average revenue generated per order |

Each KPI includes:
- Current value
- Growth percentage
- Trend sparkline

### 🧠 Dynamic Business Insights

The dashboard automatically generates executive-level insights using DAX measures.

Examples:

- Cycling is the largest sport category with ₹8.06 crore, contributing 22.0% of total revenue.
- Maharashtra generated ₹6.08 crore, contributing 16.6% of total company revenue.
- Website is the highest-performing sales channel with ₹12.30 crore.
- Wrong Item Delivered is the leading return reason with 2,416 returned orders.

These insights update automatically based on applied filters.

## 📌 Business Insights

- Cycling is the highest revenue-generating sports category.
- Maharashtra contributes the largest share of total revenue.
- Website is the most effective sales channel.
- Customer distribution is nearly balanced between male and female shoppers.
- Customer retention remains consistently above 13%.
- Wrong Item Delivered is the primary reason for product returns.
- Sales peak during the second quarter, indicating seasonal demand.
- Average Order Value exceeds ₹12K, reflecting strong customer purchasing power.
